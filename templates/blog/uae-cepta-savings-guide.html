1. What is UAE CEPA and Why Does It Matter?

CEPA (Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement) is a free trade agreement between India and the UAE signed in February 2022, effective 1 May 2022. Under CEPA, India and the UAE agreed to reduce or eliminate customs duties on over 90% of goods traded between the two countries.

For Indian exporters, this means: if you're exporting to the UAE, you should be paying 0% customs duty on most products — not the standard BCD (Basic Customs Duty) rate that can be as high as 10%, 20%, or even 30%.

The Problem

Most Indian exporters — especially SMEs — don't know UAE CEPA exists, or don't know their products qualify. They pay full duty unnecessarily. Studies estimate Indian exporters collectively overpay $4-8 billion per year in avoidable customs duties.

3. How to Claim UAE CEPA — Step by Step

Step 1: Get Your IEC + Importer-Exporter Code

You need a valid IEC (Import Export Code) issued by DGFT. Apply at dgft.gov.in — it's free and takes 1-5 working days.

Step 2: Classify Your Product with the Correct HS Code

The HS code determines your duty rate. A wrong HS code = wrong duty rate = customs delays. Use ExportPilot's HS Classifier to find the correct 6-8 digit code for your product in under 30 seconds.

Step 3: Check if Your Product Qualifies for CEPA

Use ExportPilot's FTA Optimizer to check if your HS code gets 0% duty under UAE CEPA. Enter your HS code and cargo value — it shows all available FTAs and estimated savings.

Step 4: Get a Certificate of Origin (CoO)

This is the document that proves your goods are made in India. For UAE CEPA, you need a Certificate of Origin stating the goods qualify under CEPA rules of origin (usually "Change of Chapter"). You can generate this using ExportPilot's Document Generator.

Step 5: File Bill of Entry at UAE Customs

When your goods arrive in the UAE, your freight forwarder files the Bill of Entry. Attach the Certificate of Origin and claim CEPA preferential rate. The UAE customs officer verifies and approves the 0% duty.

4. 5 Mistakes Indian Exporters Make with UAE CEPA

✗
Not knowing CEPA exists — Most SME exporters in India have never heard of UAE CEPA. They pay full duty unknowingly.
✗
Wrong HS code — A wrong 4-digit or 6-digit HS code can mean you're charged the wrong (higher) duty rate.
✗
No Certificate of Origin — Even if your product qualifies for 0%, you can't claim it without a valid CoO.
✗
Using wrong FTA — Some products get 0% under UAE CEPA but NOT under ASEAN or other FTAs. Know your best route.
✗
Not verifying rules of origin — CEPA requires "substantial transformation." If your product doesn't meet the rule of origin (usually Change of Chapter), it won't qualify even if the HS code matches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can claim UAE CEPA benefits?

Any Indian exporter with a valid IEC exporting goods to the UAE. Both the exporter (in India) and the importer (in UAE) can claim the benefit — it reduces cost for the UAE buyer too.

Is there a minimum shipment value for CEPA?

No minimum value. Even small shipments benefit from 0% duty under CEPA.

How long does a Certificate of Origin take?

You can generate one instantly using ExportPilot. For government-certified CoOs (required for some products), your local Chamber of Commerce or FIEO can issue them in 1-2 days.

Does UAE CEPA cover services?

Yes — CEPA covers both goods and services. India's services exports to UAE (IT, consultancy, hospitality) also benefit from preferential treatment.

What happens if UAE customs rejects my CoO?

If your CoO is rejected, you'll be charged the standard MFN duty rate. Keep records of your manufacturing process to defend your claim if challenged.
