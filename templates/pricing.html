Common Questions

Is the free tier really free? What's the catch?

Yes, 5 HS lookups and 3 documents per month — no credit card needed. We want every Indian exporter to at least know their correct HS code. The paid plans are for people who export regularly and want unlimited access.

How does payment work?

We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer, and card payments. After subscribing, you'll receive a payment link via email/WhatsApp within 24 hours. Your account is activated immediately after payment confirmation.

Is my trade data kept private?

100% private. Your HS codes, export destinations, and product descriptions are never shared with anyone. We don't sell data. Privacy policy coming soon.

How accurate is the HS code classification?

Our database covers 40+ major Indian export categories with 85-95% accuracy for common products. For specialised goods, we show the most likely codes with a confidence score. Always verify with a customs broker for critical shipments.

I'm a customs broker / CA. Can I white-label this?

Yes! Business plan includes white-label for your clients. Contact us for custom pricing for broker firms. We also offer API access for integration with your existing systems.

Do you guarantee duty savings?

We show you the rates and FTAs — the savings depend on correctly applying them. Our data is sourced from published government tariffs and trade agreements. We recommend verifying with a licensed customs broker for high-value shipments.
